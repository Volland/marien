Why Marien

Rules next to the data they judge

Your compliance checks, integrity constraints and reasoning live in the database as modules, checked against the schema they read, and run where the data is.

λ

Typed by the schema

Every table and property becomes a type and a predicate. A misspelled property or a string compared with a date fails when you save, with its line and column.

⊢

Validation as a protocol

Any module that declares violation joins elpi_validate: one report of severity, code, message and the node at fault.

∂

Analysed before it runs

Marien works out what a rule reads, whether it is monotone and how far it looks from its subject, and loads only that.

▦

Partitioned at scale

Rules that stay near their subject run batch by batch, in fresh stores. Lean proves no subject is checked twice or missed.

In thirty seconds

Know-your-customer checks, as rules

A beneficial-ownership graph of people, companies and accounts, and the checks a compliance team runs on it. The full walk-through is in the tour.

kyc.elpi
accumulate graph.std.

pred violation o:severity, o:string, o:subject.

% 25% or more, and KYC never completed
violation error "unverified-ubo" (subj_person P) :-
  shareholder_of P E _, shareholder_of.share E S,
  S >= 25.0, not (person.kyc_verified P _).

% companies owning each other in a circle
violation error "circular-ownership" (subj_company C) :-
  acyclic_witness_in in_cycle.owns C.

% politically exposed account holders
violation warning "pep-account" (subj_account A) :-
  holds (holds.src.person P) _ (holds.dst.account A),
  person.pep P tt.

How it works

One extension, one Lean runtime

A thin C++ shim reads LadybugDB's catalog and tables in your transaction. Everything else is Lean: the schema encoding, the analyses, the partition planner, and the LeanElpi engine that runs your rules.

1

Save

elpi_save typechecks the module against the signature generated from the schema, analyses it and stores it in _elpi_module.

2

Load

A call loads the facts its entry point can reach, from full scope, a narrowed scope or one partition batch.

3

Solve

LeanElpi searches in fuel slices, so cancellation, timeouts and budgets end the call with an error, never a partial answer.

4

Return

Answers come back as typed rows that Cypher can MATCH on, filter and join with the rest of the graph.
